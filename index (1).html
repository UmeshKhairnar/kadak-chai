Questions shop owners ask

 

Can I import my existing customers and items?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet of customers or items and Bahi maps the columns for you. Most files import in under a minute.

Do I need a GST number to use Bahi?

No. Without a GSTIN you can issue plain bills. Add your GSTIN later in Settings and tax invoices start working from the next bill.

Who owns my data?

You do. Export all invoices, customers and stock to a spreadsheet at any time, on any plan.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your account moves to the free Starter plan. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade again whenever you like.
